# Revigate CLI V4 Public Test Rulebook

## R-01: Eligibility
Participants qualify after maintaining an active public enrollment record.

## R-02: Benefit
Qualified participants receive access to the published test materials.

## R-03: Evidence
Eligibility is demonstrated by a dated public enrollment record.

## R-04: Appeals
A participant may request a manual review within seven days of a decision.
