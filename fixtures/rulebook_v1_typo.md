## R-01: Eligibility

## R-02: Benefit
A qualifying participant may receive a fixed participant grant as separately promised in their signed on-chain offer.

A participant must remain enrolled for at least 14 days before requesting the program benefit.

## R-04: Evidence
The program may ask for a publicly viewable enrollment record; private personal information is not required.

## R-05: Notice
Any change to the rules will be published as a new, version-addressed Markdown rulebook.

## R-06: Prior funded rights
Previously accepted, funded on-chain payment rights remain governed by the frozen signed offer, regardless of later rule revisions.

## R-07: Exit
A participant whose accepted program rules materially change may use only the financial exit option expressly frozen in their separate on-chain offer.
